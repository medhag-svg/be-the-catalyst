# Living Portal — Exhibition Setup

## Physical layout

1. Place the Kinect centrally below or above the projected image.
2. Keep its view clear and face it directly toward visitors.
3. Mark a comfortable interaction zone about 2–3.5 metres from the sensor.
4. Keep strong sunlight away from the Kinect depth camera.
5. Use Windows **Extend these displays** mode. The launcher automatically chooses the first non-primary display as the projector.

## Start

1. Switch on the projector and confirm Windows detects it.
2. Connect Kinect power and USB 3.0.
3. Double-click `RUN_LIVING_PORTAL.bat`.
4. Wait approximately five seconds. The projection opens full-screen automatically.
5. Stand in the interaction zone. Press `H` temporarily to confirm `KINECT LIVE` and the body count.

## First installation calibration

1. Stand centrally with the full body visible.
2. Press `C` to open calibration.
3. Use arrow keys until the projected portal aligns with the body.
4. Use `[` and `]` if the silhouette is too large or small.
5. Press `M` if left and right are reversed.
6. Press `C` again to save and close. Settings persist after restart.

## Normal experience

- The molecular field appears when the space is empty.
- A visitor becomes a procedural living portal surrounded by six kinds of enlarged atoms.
- An open palm attracts nearby elements without collecting them.
- Closing the fist near one atom and holding for half a second collects exactly that atom.
- Bringing both hands together—or joining another visitor's hand—tests the collected combination.
- Lowering a hand below the hip for one second releases all atoms held by that hand. The Kinect lasso hand gesture also releases them.
- Eight valid molecular combinations create different full-screen graphics and sound.
- Stillness reveals finer bonds in the surrounding field.

## Recovery

- If the picture is on the wrong screen, stop the installation, confirm Windows Extend mode, and run it again.
- If the Kinect indicator is not live, reconnect the Kinect to a rear/direct USB 3.0 port and restart.
- If tracking is reversed, press `M`.
- If alignment changes, press `C` and recalibrate.
- If visual memory becomes too dense, press `R`.
- Use `RUN_DEMO_WITHOUT_KINECT.bat` to verify the projector and graphics without the sensor.

## Stop

Double-click `STOP_LIVING_PORTAL.bat`. It closes only the Living Portal server, Kinect bridge, and projection window.
